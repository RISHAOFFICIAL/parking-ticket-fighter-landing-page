Detroit Discount

DETROIT RESIDENTS: 50% OFF YOUR TICKETS

City of Detroit program

Detroit’s Detroit Discount program cuts Expired Meter and No Parking fines in half. Register your license plate once and the discount applies.

  • Your plate Your plate must be registered in the City of Detroit.
  • Your record You must have no more than one unpaid ticket on your record, including the current one.
  • Pay fast The ticket must be paid within 5 calendar days of issue.

Registration takes about 60 minutes to process.

Register my plate for the Detroit Discount

This is the City of Detroit’s program, not ours. Registering does not contest a ticket. It is for paying one at half price. To contest a ticket, file for Administrative Review instead.

Cross-examination

Questions, answered straight

No fine print
Is it really free? +

Yes. Free during launch. Photographing your ticket, the case check, the statement, and the full contest packet cost nothing.

What if I did not pay for parking? +

The app still checks your case. Signs, markings, meter faults, and errors on the ticket itself can all matter. And if your case is weak, it tells you that straight, so you can pay the $22.50 before it becomes $45.

How long does it take? +

About 10 minutes, most of it photographing the ticket and gathering your evidence. The reading, the math, and the drafting are done for you.

What happens after I file? +

Detroit mails the result in about 30 days. Penalties are frozen while your contest is under review, so the fine does not grow while you wait.

Is this legal advice? +

No. It is guidance: your ticket read back to you, your deadlines computed, your statement drafted from your facts, and the city’s own filing steps laid out. The decision to contest is yours.
